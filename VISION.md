# VISION

## The idea

**Everyone's social media has value. What's your price?**

WhoAmI by The Agency is a marketplace where people can openly price access to their social distribution and where brands, businesses, organizations, and other creators can discover them and make offers.

The platform starts from one belief:

> Everyone is already an influencer to someone.

Influence is not a badge granted after reaching a follower threshold. A small trusted audience can be economically useful, especially when it is local, specialized, engaged, or difficult to reach through conventional advertising.

## What WhoAmI by The Agency should make easy

Today a collaboration can require discovery, cold outreach, email threads, negotiation, contracts, asset exchange, verification, payment, and proof of completion.

WhoAmI by The Agency should compress that workflow into:

**Discover → Offer → Accept / Counter → Create → Verify → Pay**

## Creator-side model

Every user should be able to build a public storefront for their reach.

A profile can eventually contain:
- social identities
- audience size
- niche or interests
- geography
- engagement signals
- portfolio
- availability
- reputation
- completed collaborations
- repeat partners
- pricing

A creator chooses their own rates.

The market may eventually provide contextual pricing intelligence, but the platform should never dictate whether a person is "big enough" to participate.

## Brand-side model

A buyer should be able to discover people by useful criteria such as:
- platform
- niche
- geography
- price
- audience size
- engagement
- availability
- verification
- past performance

The buyer can purchase at a listed rate or make an offer.

Creators can accept, counter, or decline.

## Pricing primitives

WhoAmI by The Agency should support both transactional and recurring relationships.

### One-off

Examples:
- sponsored post
- story
- short-form video
- mention
- link placement
- newsletter placement
- custom deliverable

### Recurring

Examples:
- monthly ambassador relationship
- recurring posts
- recurring stories
- ongoing product partnership
- custom monthly package

This allows a business to build a portfolio of smaller creators instead of relying on a single large account.

## Everyone is premium

WhoAmI by The Agency should not have conventional premium feature gates.

Core product capabilities should remain available to everyone:
- profile creation
- pricing
- discovery
- offers
- negotiation
- transactions
- reputation
- marketplace participation

Revenue should come from providing real value, not deliberately degrading the free experience.

## Verification is a service

Verification is different from premium.

Verification requires real labor and creates real trust. Manual verification is therefore a legitimate paid service and a source of margin.

Possible verification classes may eventually include:
- identity verified
- social account ownership verified
- business verified
- audience reviewed
- payment identity verified
- portfolio or historical work reviewed

Verification should communicate confidence, not privilege.

A user must not need verification to participate in the marketplace.

## Open source

The software is licensed under GPLv3.

The hosted WhoAmI by The Agency marketplace may still create value through:
- marketplace transaction economics
- human verification
- payment infrastructure
- dispute handling
- trust and safety
- hosted convenience
- network effects
- marketplace liquidity
- future business services

Open software and a commercially sustainable hosted marketplace are compatible.

## Long-term opportunity

As transactions accumulate, WhoAmI by The Agency may become a source of real market price discovery for social distribution.

Instead of asking:

> What should an influencer with 10,000 followers charge?

the marketplace can eventually answer questions grounded in actual completed deals:

> What are buyers actually paying for a technology-focused LinkedIn post from creators with 1,000–5,000 followers in the United States?

That creates a new layer of intelligence on top of the marketplace.

## Non-negotiables

1. Everyone can participate.
2. Everyone gets the core tools.
3. Creators control their listed price.
4. Verification is trust, not a paywall.
5. Small audiences are first-class inventory.
6. The software remains open source under GPLv3.
7. The product should reduce outreach friction rather than recreate it inside another app.
